🔒 How Cloud-to-Local Privacy Works

When you point LocalTube to a folder on your computer, your browser reads the files directly from your local disk (FileSystemDirectoryHandle / Blob URL) and stores thumbnails, actor tags, and watch history in your local browser IndexedDB. No video or metadata is ever transmitted to any server.
